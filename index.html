About Alvin

A partner for the long game

With over two decades of experience in resilience planning, systems innovation, and development management, I partner with governments, NGOs, and communities to design strategies that last.

My work sits at the intersection of climate adaptation, disaster risk reduction, and organizational performance — disciplines that are usually kept in separate silos. I bring them together so that a strategy built for a ministry, an NGO field office, or an investment committee can survive the thing it was actually designed for: a typhoon, a funding gap, a change in leadership, a market shock.

That approach has taken me from barangay-level DRR protocols to the lead architecture of the Bangsamoro Investment Roadmap (BIRD) 2026–2035, and from WHO-partnered emergency-responder training to authoring two books on resilience — including Personal Resilience: The Path to Oneness. Whatever the mandate, the goal is the same: systems that keep working long after the workshop ends and the contract closes.

Personal Resilience: The Path to Oneness (2025) introduces the CASE Equation — a systems-based, measurable framework for resilience under compounding crisis. CASE stands for Coping capacity, Adaptability, Sensitivity reduction, and Exposure control. It translates abstract resilience concepts into diagnosable, trainable, and trackable competencies for individuals, teams, and organizations.

Use the CASE Equation in practice

ResilienceOne complements the book by helping you move from a conceptual framework to repeatable reflection and action.

C · Coping Capacity

Strengthen your ability to respond effectively to immediate challenges without becoming overwhelmed.

A · Adaptability

Build the capacity to learn, flex and grow when circumstances change.

S · Sensitivity Reduction

Notice patterns of reactivity and develop healthier ways to regulate responses to stressors.

E · Exposure Control

Identify environmental, relational and contextual influences you can reduce, redesign or manage.

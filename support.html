Common customer questions

These are representative real-world support questions, presented as an FAQ. They are not presented as verbatim historical customer tickets.

How do I request a consulting or advisory engagement?

Send a concise description of the problem, expected outcome, organization or project context, preferred timeframe, and any relevant terms of reference. The support channel can route the request for an appropriate response.

Can I request a short-term technical review or workshop?

Yes. Focused technical reviews, strategic workshops, facilitation, and advisory assignments can be discussed alongside longer-term engagements, subject to scope, availability, and requirements.

Can you work with government agencies, NGOs, development partners, or private organizations?

Yes. The professional portfolio describes work across government, development, humanitarian, resilience, planning, and institutional-strengthening contexts. Provide the mandate and expected deliverable when contacting support.

How can I obtain a copy of a report, publication, or portfolio item?

Identify the title or page where you found the item and explain what you need. If a public download exists, support can direct you to it; otherwise, access depends on the item's publication and distribution status.

I found a broken link or a page that displays code instead of the website. What should I do?

Please send the page address, a short description of what you expected to see, and—if possible—a screenshot. This makes it possible to reproduce and isolate the issue quickly.

I cannot read some text or use a page with a keyboard or screen reader. Can I report that?

Absolutely. Accessibility issues are welcome and should include the page address, the affected control or section, the assistive technology or browser involved when known, and what happened.

How do I raise a privacy or personal-data concern?

Use the support email and mark the subject as “Privacy Request” or “Privacy Concern.” Avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials in the initial message.

What information should I include in a support request?

Include the affected page or service, what you were trying to accomplish, what happened, when it happened, your browser or device when relevant, and any non-sensitive error message. Do not include passwords, OTPs, API keys, or other secrets.
